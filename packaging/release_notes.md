The sprint 4 combat sandbox: fight wave after wave of enemies in the asteroid field. The flight test and the weapon gallery are in the same game.

**Download:** `BitMiner-CombatSandbox-windows.zip` below. Unzip it anywhere (Desktop or Downloads is fine), open the `BitMiner` folder and double-click `BitMiner.exe`.

If Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The game isn't signed with a publisher certificate yet, so Windows doesn't recognise it.

**Menu:** Combat Sandbox is picked to start with. Use the mouse or the arrow keys and Enter. Esc goes back to the menu from anywhere.

**Combat sandbox:** WASD flies, the mouse aims (the view leans toward it), click fires. 1 to 4, the mouse wheel, or Q and E change weapon. N calls the next wave early, P pauses, Backspace starts a fresh fight. Waves arrive on a timer whether or not you have cleared the last one: infantry squads hold formation around you and shoot, guards wall them off and ram you when things go badly (watch for the flashing lane), and rapid assault ships wait behind you and dive past. Your ship now bumps enemies and asteroids, rocks block shots, missiles and the laser, your shield recharges after a few quiet seconds, and the laser has 3 charges that slowly come back. If you go down you are back a moment later. Each fight is written to a `playdata` folder next to `BitMiner.exe` for the adaptive AI in a later sprint.

**Weapon gallery:** as before, now with a visible edge where free-fly stops you, and your hull bumps theirs.

**Flight test:** W main booster, S retro, A and D strafe, mouse to aim. R puts the ship back at the start.

**Everywhere:** Tab shows or hides the tuning panel, C toggles the CRT filter, F11 is full screen. Your tuning saves to `flight_tuning.json` next to `BitMiner.exe`. There is one new slider, CAMERA AIM, for how far the view leans toward the mouse in a fight.

This build is made automatically from the latest code.
