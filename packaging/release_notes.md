The sprint 3 weapon gallery: every weapon against every enemy, and every enemy attack against you, playing on a loop. The sprint 2 flight test is in the same game.

**Download:** `BitMiner-WeaponGallery-windows.zip` below. Unzip it anywhere (Desktop or Downloads is fine), open the `BitMiner` folder and double-click `BitMiner.exe`.

If Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The game isn't signed with a publisher certificate yet, so Windows doesn't recognise it.

**Menu:** pick Flight Test or Weapon Gallery with the mouse or the arrow keys and Enter. Esc goes back to the menu from either one.

**Weapon gallery:** Left and Right pick a weapon or an enemy attack, Up and Down pick the target or your defense (shield, shield breaking, or straight onto the armor). You can also click the list on the right. Space replays the scene. F switches between the auto pilot and free-fly, where you fly with WASD, aim with the mouse, click to fire and press 1 to 4 to change weapon.

**Flight test:** W main booster, S retro, A and D strafe, mouse to aim. R puts the ship back at the start.

**Everywhere:** Tab shows or hides the tuning panel, C toggles the CRT filter, F11 is full screen. Your tuning saves to `flight_tuning.json` next to `BitMiner.exe`; the starting values are the ones you sent back after the flight test.

This build is made automatically from the latest code.
